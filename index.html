Questions, answered honestly.

Is it safe to give Merit my Schoology password?
Your password is stored only in your device's encrypted Keychain and is sent only to Schoology, never to us and never to anyone else. It signs you in the same way the Schoology website does. Merit's optional account (Sign in with Apple, used for Ask Merit credits) never receives it.
What does Ask Merit actually know about me?
Only what you allow. It sees your course names, and for the courses you choose to share, your grades, assignments, and relevant class materials. It asks your permission before the first question, and you can change which courses it reads, or turn it off, in Settings.
Does Merit work with my school?
Merit works with schools that sign in to Schoology with an email and password. You enter your school's ID once, so it isn't limited to a single campus. If your school uses a single sign-on provider instead of a Schoology password, that isn't supported yet.
Is it really free?
Merit Dock is free to download, with no ads. Ask Merit runs on credits: you get complimentary credits to start and can buy more in the app. There's no business model built on your data: no tracking, no analytics, nothing sold. It's an app built by a student, for students.
Can you see my grades?
Not unless you use Ask Merit. Your grades are fetched straight from Schoology to your device and cached there. If you ask Merit a question, it first asks your permission, then sends your question and the school info you allow to Merit's AI service to write the answer. That information is erased as soon as your answer is ready.
Is Merit affiliated with Schoology?
No. Merit is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Schoology or PowerSchool. Schoology is a trademark of its respective owner. You need your own school Schoology login to use Merit.
How do I delete my data?
Sign out in Settings to remove your saved login and cached school data from the device, or simply delete the app. If you created a Merit account, you can delete it in Settings too. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is kept and for how long.
