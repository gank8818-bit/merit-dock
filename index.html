Why you can trust it

Your login never leaves your Mac.

Merit is a third-party app that signs in with your own Schoology account — so where your password goes is the only question that matters. Here's the whole path, with nothing hidden.

You Type your Schoology email & password
Your Mac's Keychain Encrypted, on this device only
Schoology Over HTTPS, the same site you already use
Never sent to our servers — because there are none. Merit has no account and no backend.

Stored in the Keychain, nowhere else

Your credentials sit in Apple's encrypted Keychain and are sent only to Schoology — the same place your browser sends them. We never see them.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

There are no third-party SDKs, no telemetry, and no data brokers. Nothing about you is measured, sold, or shipped anywhere.

Lock it behind Face ID

Turn on App Lock and your grades stay behind Touch ID, Face ID, or your Mac passcode — one more layer that's entirely yours.

Everything in one window

Merit reads your Schoology account the way the website does — then organizes it into the view you actually wanted.

Every course's overall grade, the category breakdown, and each graded item — as a percentage or a standards-based rubric, whichever your school uses.

Straight answers

Questions, answered honestly.

Is it safe to give Merit my Schoology password?
Your password is stored only in your Mac's encrypted Keychain and is sent only to Schoology — never to us, and never to anyone else. Merit has no server and no account of its own, so there is nowhere else for it to go. It signs you in the same way the Schoology website does.
Does Merit work with my school?
Merit works with schools that sign in to Schoology with an email and password. You enter your school's ID once, so it isn't limited to a single campus. If your school uses a single sign-on provider instead of a Schoology password, that isn't supported yet.
Is it really free? What's the catch?
It's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. There's no catch because there's no business model built on your data — no tracking, no analytics, nothing sold. It's an app built by a student, for students.
Can you see my grades?
No. Your grades are fetched straight from Schoology to your Mac and cached on your device. They never pass through any server we control, because we don't run one.
Is Merit affiliated with Schoology?
No. Merit is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Schoology or PowerSchool. Schoology is a trademark of its respective owner. You need your own school Schoology login to use Merit.
Is there an iPhone and iPad version?
Yes — Merit is a universal app that also runs natively on iPhone and iPad, so your grades follow you across your devices.
