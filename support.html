Support

Merit Dock for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Need a hand?

  • Email penguin.profiles@gmail.com.
  • Or use Send Feedback in the app's Settings. Add your email if you'd like a reply.
  • Please never send us your Schoology password. We will never ask for it.

Signing in

How do I connect my Schoology account?

Open Settings, enter the email and password you use for Schoology, and your school's ID if asked, then connect (on Mac, click Save & Reconnect). Your grades and assignments load in a few seconds.

My school signs in with Google, Microsoft, or another single sign-on

Merit currently works with schools that sign in to Schoology with an email and password. Single sign-on isn't supported yet.

On Mac it says “Not initialized”

This was a bug in version 2.0.1 on Mac that stopped your password from saving. Update to Merit Dock 2.5 or later from the App Store, then enter your Schoology password again and click Save & Reconnect.

Grades and assignments

Something is missing or out of date

Pull down to refresh on iPhone and iPad, or use the refresh button on Mac. Merit shows what Schoology shows your account, including work your teachers have posted but not yet added to the gradebook. Your school's Schoology is always the official record.

Where did my class timetable go?

Schoology doesn't provide class times, so the timetable is entered by hand and is hidden unless you turn it on: Settings → Appearance → Show class schedule on iPhone and iPad, or Settings → Show class schedule on Mac.

Ask Merit

What does Ask Merit share?

Before your first question, Ask Merit explains what it sends and asks for permission. It sends your question and the school info you allow, and only courses you choose to share include grades, assignments, and materials. Details are in the Privacy Policy. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

How do credits work?

Each Ask Merit request uses credits. You get complimentary credits to start, and you can buy more in the app. Purchases are processed by Apple.

How do I redeem a code?

In Ask Merit, open the credits menu and choose Redeem Code. Redeeming requires Sign in with Apple so your balance can be restored on your other devices.

A purchase didn't show up

Open Ask Merit again while online; Merit checks for completed purchases automatically. If credits are still missing after a few minutes, email us with the date of the purchase. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Your data

How do I remove my information?

  • From your device: use Reconnect School Account or sign out in Settings to remove your saved sign-in and cached school data, or delete the app.
  • Your Merit account: Settings → Delete Merit Account. Your identity is removed; purchase and credit records are kept without it where required for accounting and refunds.
  • Anything else: email us and we'll take care of it.
