Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026 · Applies to Merit Dock (“Merit”) on iPhone, iPad, and Mac · Publisher: Chen Ye Gan (“we”, “us”)

The short version

  • Your Schoology email and password are stored in your device's Keychain and sent only to Schoology. We never receive them.
  • Your grades, assignments, and course materials are fetched from Schoology directly to your device and cached there.
  • Ask Merit is optional. It sends your question and the school info you allow to Merit's AI service only after you give permission, and Merit's servers erase that information as soon as your answer is ready.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.

1. Information that stays on your device

Merit keeps the following only on your device. We do not receive it.

  • Schoology sign-in. Your Schoology email, password, and School ID, stored in Apple's encrypted Keychain. They are sent only to Schoology over HTTPS to sign you in, the same way the Schoology website does.
  • School data. Grades, assignments, tests, course materials, attachments, and your Schoology name and profile picture, cached so Merit opens instantly and works offline.
  • Your study work. Ask Merit conversations, saved quizzes, flashcards, and study guides.
  • Preferences. Theme, notifications, App Lock, which courses Ask Merit may read, your class timetable if you enter one, and your AI permission choice.
  • Widgets. On iPhone and iPad, a small summary for Home Screen widgets, stored in the app's private container on the device.

2. Ask Merit (optional)

Ask Merit is an AI study assistant. Before your first request, Merit explains what it shares and asks your permission. Nothing is sent until you allow it, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

What is sent

  • Your question and the recent messages in that conversation.
  • Your course names and, if you use one, your class timetable.
  • For courses you choose to share only: grades, assignments, and excerpts of course materials relevant to your question.

Your Schoology password, session cookies, and payment details are never sent.

Where it goes

Requests go over HTTPS to Merit's AI service, which forwards them to a third-party AI model provider that processes them only to write your answer, under that provider's service terms.

How long it is kept

  • Your question and the school info sent with it are erased from Merit's servers as soon as your answer is ready.
  • The answer is cleared from Merit's servers within 24 hours. Your copy stays on your device.
  • We keep a usage record for each request (time, AI model, amount of text processed, and credits charged) for billing, support, and abuse prevention. It does not contain your question or answer.

Please don't put personal information into a question that you don't want an AI model to read.

3. Your Merit account and credits

Ask Merit uses credits. To keep your balance safe, Merit's service stores:

  • An account identifier. Either a one-way hash of your App Store installation identity, or, if you use Sign in with Apple, the identifier Apple provides and, if you choose to share it, your email address (which can be an Apple private-relay address).
  • Your credit ledger. Credits granted, spent, purchased, or redeemed.
  • Purchases. The App Store transaction and product identifiers needed to credit a purchase exactly once. Apple processes payment; we never receive your card details.
  • Codes. Which redemption or invite codes your account used, and for invite codes, which account's code was redeemed, so each code is credited once.

4. Feedback you send

If you use Send Feedback, the message and, only if you add one, your email address are stored so we can read it and reply. Feedback is not used for advertising.

5. What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information or share it with data brokers.
  • We do not collect location, contacts, photos, health data, or advertising identifiers.

7. Security

All connections use HTTPS with certificate validation. Sign-in details are kept in the Keychain. You can turn on App Lock to require Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode to open Merit.

8. Your choices and deletion

  • Turn off Ask Merit in Settings (Security & Privacy on iPhone and iPad, AI data privacy on Mac), and choose which courses it may read.
  • Sign out of Schoology in Settings to remove your saved sign-in and cached school data from the device. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.
  • Delete your Merit account in Settings. Your identity data is removed. Purchase and credit-ledger records are kept without your identity where needed for accounting, refunds, and fraud prevention.
  • To request access to or deletion of feedback or other information, email penguin.profiles@gmail.com.

9. Students and children

Merit is built for students using their own school accounts, including students under 18. It contains no advertising and no tracking. Students under 13 should use Merit, and especially Ask Merit, only with a parent's, guardian's, or school's permission. If you are a parent, guardian, or school and believe a child has shared information with us that should be deleted, email us and we will delete it.
